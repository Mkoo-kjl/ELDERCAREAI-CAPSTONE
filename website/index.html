WHAT THE APP BRINGS TOGETHER

The essentials,
all in one place.

Less jumping between notes, readings, and reminders. ElderCareAI keeps the essentials in a single caregiver view.

01

Health readings, in context

Review heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep, steps, overnight skin temperature, and HRV when Google Health has synced them.

02

Care plans that stay close

Keep medications, appointments, caregiver notes, and the saved doctor's contact beside the patient's latest information.

03

Helpful questions, clear answers

Ask Elle about available care details and synced readings. Elle is an informational assistant, not a medical device or a replacement for professional advice.

04

Timely things to remember

See upcoming doses and visits, with reminders and health alerts that help caregivers know what needs attention.

ABOUT ELDERCAREAI

Made for the person doing the caring.

Caregiving means keeping track of small details that matter a great deal. We built ElderCareAI to make those details easier to see, share, and act on, without pretending an app can replace a clinician.

01

Connect a supported wearable through Google Health.

02

See available readings alongside everyday care plans.

03

Stay grounded with clear context and a human care team.
