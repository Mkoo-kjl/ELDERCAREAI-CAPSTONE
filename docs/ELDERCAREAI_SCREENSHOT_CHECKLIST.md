# ElderCareAI Screenshot Checklist

Capture these **22 screens** from the current configured mobile build and insert them beside the matching figure numbers in `ELDERCAREAI_USER_GUIDE.docx`. Use a demonstration account or conceal patient names, contact details, coordinates, messages, and health data. Capture the SOS screen before activating it. Portrait screenshots at one consistent phone size will match the supplied reference most closely.

| Figure | Screen to screenshot | How to reach it and what to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Caregiver sign in | Fresh app launch; show **Sign in with Google**. |
| 2 | Care setup introduction | First slide after first sign-in; show **Continue** and **Skip**. |
| 3 | Caregiver information | First setup form; show its fields and **Next**. |
| 4 | Older adult information | Next setup form; show basic and medical sections, with private data hidden. |
| 5 | Google Health connection | Wearable setup; show **Connect with Google Health** and **Skip for now**. |
| 6 | Phone location choice | Location setup; show **Allow phone location** and **Continue without location**. |
| 7 | Home dashboard | **Home** tab; include the older adult card, Health vitals, and Care plan if one frame permits. |
| 8 | Health vitals | **Health > Vitals**; show a sample value and its measurement time. |
| 9 | Health insights | **Health > AI Insights**; show a sample insight. |
| 10 | Health predictions | **Health > Predictions**; show a trend or pending state. |
| 11 | Health anomalies | **Health > Anomalies**; show a review item or normal state. |
| 12 | Last sync location | **Home > Last sync location**; conceal any real map coordinates. |
| 13 | Medication schedule | **Care > Medications**; show a saved sample medication and status. |
| 14 | Add medication form | **Care > Medications > plus**; show frequency and reminder choices. |
| 15 | Appointment form | **Care > Appointments > plus**; show date, time, doctor, and Save. |
| 16 | Caregiver notes | **Care > Notes**; show a saved sample note. |
| 17 | Health alerts and SOS | **Alerts > Alerts**; include the SOS button, without activating it. |
| 18 | Caregiver notifications | **Alerts > Notifications**; show a sample saved message. |
| 19 | Elle care assistant | **Elle** tab; show a demonstration question and reply. |
| 20 | Settings and doctor contact | **Settings**; show Manage Profiles, Doctor Contact, and Google Health API v4. |
| 21 | Health records | **Settings > Manage health records**; show Medical history and Checkup history. |
| 22 | Reports and permissions | Lower **Settings** screen; show Data Management and App Permissions. |

Figures 2-6 belong to first-time setup. To capture them after completing onboarding, use a dedicated demonstration account rather than changing a real patient's record. Figures 8-11 require synchronized sample readings for populated examples; an honest empty or pending state is acceptable when those data are unavailable.
