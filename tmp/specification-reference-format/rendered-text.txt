Human Resource Specification 
The research study, ElderCareAI, was designed to address the needs of the following users: 
1.  Caregiver 
The caregiver manages the assigned older adult’s profile, pairs the watch, reviews readings and 
location, maintains medications and appointments, responds to alerts, and consults Elle AI. The caregiver 
also enables notification permissions and checks whether displayed data is current. 
2.  System Administrator 
The administrator uses the web portal to manage authorized user accounts and patient records, 
review stored monitoring data and audit activity, and generate system reports. Administrative privileges 
must be restricted to approved personnel. 
3.  Older Adult / Watch Wearer 
The older adult wears the Galaxy Watch6, keeps it charged, and uses the watch’s monitoring, spot 
oxygen, SOS and medication controls as needed. The caregiver can assist with setup and permissions. A 
separate caregiver-style phone account is not required simply to wear the paired watch.

Program Specification 
The system is to be implemented with the following program specifications: 
Functional Requirements 
ElderCareAI is expected to perform the following functionalities: 
– Authentication and profiles. Provide caregiver Google sign -in and separate authenticated 
administrator access. Maintain caregiver, patient and emergency -contact details with role -based 
access. 
– Watch integration. Pair the Galaxy Watch6 with the correct patient, request the necessary 
permissions, display connection status and provide monitoring start/stop controls. 
– Health monitoring. Display supported heart rate, oxygen, wrist skin temperature, steps, sleep and 
derived stress/HRV information with units and individual measurement times. 
– Vital-event history. Save every accepted new vital event with its original time and source. Retain 
unacknowledged watch samples for retry and prevent duplicate retry records. Missing metrics remain 
blank rather than being fabricated. 
– Location tracking. Display the patient’s latest valid watch location and update the map when new 
positions arrive. Indicate location age or accuracy so an old fix is not presented as current movement. 
– Medications and appointments. Create, update and display care schedules, instructions and reminders. 
Synchronize supported medication “Taken” actions between watch and app, with clear save and 
reminder-setup feedback. 
– Threshold alerts. Evaluate valid uploaded readings against the configured app review rules. Save 
threshold warnings in Alerts and Notifications, apply duplicate/cooldown controls, and queue 
caregiver push delivery. 
– SOS and notifications. Allow a watch SOS request to create a caregiver alert. Deliver SOS, vital 
warnings, medication reminders and appointment reminders through the configured notification 
paths. SOS is an assistance request, not an automatic emergency-services dispatch. 
– Health history import. Import permitted sleep and recorded oxygen data from the selected patient 
watch through the phone’s health history integration. Retry failed uploads without inventing or 
duplicating readings. 
– Elle AI and analytics. Answer questions about saved patient history, summarize trends and provide 
clearly qualified statistical insights. Distinguish missing data from a confirmed negative result; avoid 
diagnosis or guaranteed health predictions. 
– Reports. Provide patient vital-history and medication-schedule PDF exports, together with authorized 
administrative reporting. 
– Administration and settings. Support account and record management, audit review, device status, 
notification preferences and sound settings. Expose clear permission, connectivity and 
synchronization errors.

Uses valid supported skin-contact readings. This value is wrist skin temperature,

HRV / stress level 
Rolling RMSSD HRV is calculated from valid beat intervals over about 30–60

score is invented while the baseline is insufficient. 
Steps Uses step-counter updates while monitoring. Time when monitoring is off cannot

Sleep 
Imports actual sleep sessions after they are available through the health-history 
SDK. It does not stream second by second or guarantee delivery immediately on

A qualifying reading can create a warning after upload. Repeated notices for an active live rule 
are limited to one per hour; a return to range and a new crossing reset it. Imported records use 
deduplication. Push work is triggered after database commit, with scheduled recovery. Android and 
network conditions affect delivery. 
Nonfunctional Requirements 
ElderCareAI is designed to operate under the following parameters: 
– Data quality. Validate values, timestamps and sensor quality. Mark missing or stale data clearly. 
Consumer wearable readings and derived scores must not be represented as clinical -grade 
measurements. 
– Security and privacy. Use authenticated access, role checks, database row -level policies and 
encrypted network communication. Keep privileged backend, AI and push credentials out of client 
code. 
– Reliability and integrity. Use event identifiers, acknowledgments and retries to reduce data loss and 
duplicate history. Preserve independent measurement times and source details. 
– Responsiveness. Display new accepted data and queue threshold alerts promptly. Sensor intervals, 
continuous coverage and notification arrival times cannot be guaranteed. 
– Background operation. Support Android push when the app is not open, using the correct APK, 
credentials, registration and permission. Force-stop, offline state, battery policies, muted channels and 
Do Not Disturb can affect delivery or sound. 
– Usability and compatibility. Use readable labels, units, clear controls and understandable errors. 
Validate supported Android phones, Galaxy Watch6 and modern-browser administrator access. 
– Maintainability and capacity. Keep mobile, watch and backend modules organized and testable. 
Monitor database growth, cloud quotas, failures and retry queues as the number of patients and 
recorded samples increases.
