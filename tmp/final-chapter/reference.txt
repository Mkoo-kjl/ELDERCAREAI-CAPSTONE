Findings of the Study 
The study revealed that WisEnergy, an IoT- and AI-powered household energy monitoring 
application, effectively addresses significant gaps in current household energy management 
practices in Mandaue City and Lapu -Lapu City. Using a descriptive research desi gn, the research 
team gathered insights from 418 respondents. Key findings include the need for improved 
awareness of appliance-level consumption, better budgeting and forecasting of electricity bills, and 
more accessible tools for tracking and analyzing u sage patterns. Respondents expressed strong 
agreement that features such as real-time monitoring, appliance-level reports, mobile accessibility, 
AI-generated recommendations, and timely alerts would enhance their ability to manage and reduce 
energy costs. Negative perceptions of current energy monitoring practices, coupled with high 
support for WisEnergy’s proposed features, highlight its potential to empower households to make 
informed energy decisions, optimize consumption, and reduce electricity expenses while promoting 
sustainable energy use.

CONCLUSION 
The study concludes that WisEnergy is a feasible and effective system that responds to the 
energy monitoring and management challenges faced by households in Mandaue City and Lapu -
Lapu City. Findings revealed that most respondents experience difficulties i n tracking appliance-
level consumption, managing electricity budgets, and interpreting monthly billing data. These 
results indicate a clear lack of accessible, reliable, and user-friendly tools for understanding energy 
usage in local households. The proposed WisEnergy system addresses these issues by providing a 
mobile platform that integrates IoT-based data collection with AI-powered analysis to deliver real-
time monitoring, personalized recommendations, and timely alerts. 
Survey responses showed a strong positive reception to WisEnergy’s design and features. 
Respondents rated the system highly in terms of ease of use, accuracy, and overall usefulness, 
suggesting that its implementation could significantly improve awareness, convenience, and energy 
efficiency. The platform’s simple interfa ce and visual presentation of data make it inclusive and 
practical, particularly for non -technical users and older adults. With real -time information and 
budget tracking, users can better understand their energy behavior and take immediate action to 
control costs. 
The results further demonstrate that WisEnergy has the potential to strengthen household 
participation in sustainable energy practices. By enabling users to make informed decisions and 
monitor appliance usage effectively, the system promotes both financial savings and environmental 
responsibility. It also aligns with the growing demand for digital solutions that encourage 
sustainability and smart living in Philippine communities. Continued enhancement of the system, 
including improved offline access, support for multiple users, and integration with sma rt home 
devices, will further expand its usability and long-term impact. 
The study concludes that WisEnergy is a household -focused energy management system 
that integrates AI and IoT technologies to make monitoring simpler, raise awareness, and support 
informed decision-making in electricity consumption. Designed for residents of Mandaue City and 
Lapu-Lapu City, the system improves daily energy management through real -time appliance 
tracking, budget alerts, and personalized recommendations that encourage efficient and responsible 
energy use. With deliberate attention to accuracy , usability, and accessibility, WisEnergy 
demonstrates strong potential to help households better understand and control their energy 
consumption, leading to greater cost savings and promoting sustainable living within the 
community.

RECOMMENDATIONS 
These recommendations aim to guide future researchers and developers in enhancing or 
expanding WisEnergy, drawing from the insights gathered during the study. They provide a 
foundation for continued innovation in household energy monitoring and management: 
• Enable integration with smart home ecosystems  – Allow WisEnergy to connect with 
existing smart home assistants and IoT devices for automated control of appliances based 
on real-time energy data. 
• Offer incentive and reward systems: Partner with local utilities or government programs 
to give discounts, vouchers, or recognition to users who consistently meet energy -saving 
goals. 
• Multi-user household accounts: Allow different family members to access the app with 
role-based permissions, encouraging shared responsibility for energy use. 
• Allow multiple property management: Provide an option for users to manage multiple 
households or properties (e.g., a primary home and a rented unit) under a single account to 
broaden WisEnergy’s applicability for homeowners, landlords, and property managers. 
• Expand educational resources:  Include in-app guides, energy -saving tips, and tutorial 
videos to help users understand appliance consumption and the long -term benefits of 
efficient energy management. 
• Implement dark mode and accessibility options:  Offer a dark mode for visual comfort 
and integrate accessibility features such as larger fonts, simple icons, and guided tooltips 
for elderly users or those with limited technical experience. 
• Support offline functionality:  Allow limited offline features , such as viewing cached 
reports or recent energy summaries, so that users in areas with unstable internet connection, 
especially in provincial settings, can still access essential information. 
• Add gamification and motivational features:  Introduce leaderboards, progress badges, 
or streaks to make energy-saving goals engaging. Gamified feedback can motivate users to 
maintain low consumption habits.

TRANSLATIONAL RESEARCH
The primary objective of WisEnergy  is to enhance household energy efficiency and 
encourage responsible electricity consumption through the use of a mobile application integrated 
with Internet of Things (IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies. The system enables 
users to monitor appliance-level energy usage in real time, receive alerts on high consumption, and 
access data -driven recommendations for reducing electricity costs. By providing accurate, 
transparent, and user -friendly information, WisEnergy supports households in makin g informed 
energy decisions and adopting sustainable consumption practices. 
The initial implementation phase will focus on selected households within Mandaue City 
and Lapu-Lapu City. This pilot deployment will evaluate the system’s functionality, reliability, and 
user acceptability while gathering feedback to refine its technical and interface components. 
Insights obtained from this phase will guide subsequent improvements to ensure that the system 
remains responsive to the energy management needs of local communities and adaptable to 
different residential contexts. 
WisEnergy’s key features include real -time monitoring of electricity usage, budget 
management and bill estimation based on VECO and MECO rates, and AI -generated 
recommendations for optimizing consumption. The system also issues alerts when usage 
approaches preset budget limits or when irregular consumption patterns are detected. Periodic 
testing, performance evaluation, and software updates will be conducted to maintain accuracy, 
efficiency, and system stability over time. 
As WisEnergy expands, the researchers will continue to refine its design and improve its 
analytical capabilities to ensure it remains responsive to evolving user needs while advancing local 
and environmental sustainability goals. This continuous development process will guide the system 
toward becoming a model for community -based energy management solutions that contribute to 
both household savings and broader environmental well-being.
