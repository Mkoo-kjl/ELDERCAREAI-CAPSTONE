(FOR ELDERLY)ElderCareAI: An AI-Driven IoT System for Elderly Monitoring and Caregiver Support

Dear Participants,

We extend our warm invitation to you to participate in a survey to gather valuable insights into your experiences and needs concerning elderly health monitoring and caregiving practices at the Home of the Aged in Metro Cebu. This survey is conducted as part of our study on ElderCareAI, which aims to develop a smartwatch-based health monitoring system integrated with an AI-powered mobile application for caregivers.

The questionnaire should require approximately 5–10 minutes of your time to complete.

Your participation matters:

Confidentiality and Anonymity: Rest assured that all your responses will be STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL and ANONYMOUS.

 Data Security: The research team will be the sole entity accessing the survey data. Your privacy is our utmost priority.

 No Personal Identifiers: No personal names or identifying information will be used throughout our analysis.

Your insights are invaluable in helping us design a system that will improve the quality of care for elderly individuals. Your feedback will contribute significantly to our ongoing efforts to enhance elderly monitoring and caregiver support.

Survey Duration: Approximately 5–10 minutes.

Confidentiality: Your responses are strictly confidential and anonymous.

Access: The survey is accessible to the chosen participants.

Your cooperation in this survey is deeply appreciated. Thank you for investing your time and effort in assisting us in improving elderly health monitoring and caregiver support.

Warm regards,

The Research Team
